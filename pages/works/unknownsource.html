Subject to the sonic address of an unknown source
Performance & Installation
Jason Clark & Xico Ribas
Shown at Treehouse NDSM, part of the Sensory Dialogues: Attuning Our Bodies group exhibition
May - June, 2025

Subject to the sonic address of an unknown source is an installation that plays with the subtle; it triggers a change in the audience’s listening awareness without directly addressing them. This site specific installation aims to create an instability of how we listen to art exhibitions, and how we sonically engage and participate in such spaces. This is achieved by encouraging sonic leakages and augmentations from both the inside and outside of the pavilion. Our expected perception of the space is subtly transformed.

The sonic characteristics of this installation are achieved through the placement of different microphones and transducers, which we use to relocalise/contextualise live sounds. This set-up allows us to experiment with its apparent size, as well as its materiality and/or tonal balance.
